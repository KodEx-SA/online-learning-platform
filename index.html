What are Hiragana and Katakana?

Japanese uses three writing systems. Hiragana (curved characters) represents native Japanese words and grammatical particles. Katakana (angular characters) is used for foreign loanwords, onomatopoeia, and emphasis. Together they form the two kana syllabaries — each with ~46 base characters plus voiced/combination variants.
