Japanese Kana Comprehensive Chart

What are Hiragana and Katakana?

Hiragana is used for native Japanese words and grammatical elements, while Katakana is used for foreign words, onomatopoeia, and emphasis.

Voiced Kana (Dakuon & Handakuon)

What are Dakuon and Handakuon?

Dakuon are voiced kana with two dots (e.g., が from か), and Handakuon are semi-voiced with a circle (e.g., ぱ from は).

Combo Sounds (Yōon)

What are Yōon?

Yōon are combination sounds where a consonant kana is followed by a small ゃ, ゅ, or ょ, creating a single syllable (e.g., きゃ = kya).

Small Kana & Obsolete Kana

What are Small Kana?

Small kana like っ modify pronunciation (gemination), while ゐ and ゑ are obsolete kana used historically.
